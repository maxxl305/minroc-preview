Three questions

Three questions, then the tool is settled

The question that reaches us most often is not about price. It is: which drill bit for which rock. Answer the three questions as far as you can — “unknown” is a valid answer. The rest we settle on the phone.

4,400searches a month for “Bohrkrone” (drill bit) alone

Question01Identify the rock

Which rock are you drilling?

Cuttings, a core or the profile of the neighbouring hole are almost always enough. Four classes are enough for the selection. If you are not sure, choose “Unknown” — the consultation does not hinge on it.

Pick a class — then this space tells you what it means for bit, hammer and service life.

Minroc, Groß-Umstadt

Loose ground means the hole will not stand open.

Product group
Casing systems and overburden strings with a ring bit and casing shoe.
What to watch
Collapse in the annulus. Carbide wear is secondary here.

Soft to medium: here it is the penetration rate that counts.

Product group
Top hammer drill steel with a flat bit face, ballistic buttons.
What to watch
Quartz content. It decides whether bit life keeps what the penetration rate promises.

In hard rock it is percussion energy that decides, not feed pressure.

Product group
DTH equipment or heavy top hammer drill steel, spherical buttons.
What to watch
Jointing. It pulls the hole off line long before the bit is worn out.

Quartz is 7 on the Mohs scale — wear sets the bit life.

Product group
Spherical buttons, more flushing holes, and shorter change intervals to plan for.
What to watch
Flushing capacity. Here it is not a side issue but the lever.

Unknown is the most common case — and no obstacle.

What we need
A photo of the cuttings, a core or the profile of the neighbouring hole.
For now
We start out spherical — that layout forgives the most.

Question02Choose the drilling method

What will you drill with?

The method follows from the formation and from what is meant to happen at the bottom of the hole. Three routes cover the great majority of sites.

Choose a method — or “Still open”. Question 03 then opens.

Minroc, Groß-Umstadt

The hammer sits directly above the bit.

Sizes
DTH hammers usually from 3″ to 6″, bit and hammer always as a pair.
What we check
Air demand against your compressor — air volume and working pressure.

Turns with feed pressure instead of striking.

Tools
Rotary drilling tools, HDD tools and the matching flushing media.
What we check
Feed pressure and torque of your rig against the formation.

The casing advances with the bit — the hole stays open.

Tools
Casing systems, pipe umbrellas, anchor systems.
What we check
Whether string, feed beam and connection fit one another.

Then we decide it together.

What we need
Depth, hole diameter and the purpose of the hole.
What you get
Two proposals side by side.

On to question 03 ↓Air demand follows from hammer size and hole diameter.

Question03Bit, hammer & drill string

Which connection is on the rig?

This is where most repeat orders come apart. Thread, feed beam and bit have to match. If you do not know the connection: a photo of the threaded end is enough.

After this answer the recommendation is ready — with bit face, tool family and article group.

Minroc, Groß-Umstadt

R32 — the small connection with the least margin.

T38 — the all-rounder in blast hole drilling.

T45 — for greater depths and diameters.

T51 — the heavy connection.

DTH: not top hammer drill steel but a pair.

Connection unknown? A photo is enough.

From question 01

Bit and buttons

Ring bit with casing shoe instead of a full-face bit. The bit works ahead of the casing; here collapse sets the pace.

Flat bit, ballistic buttons. Fast penetration in sandstone and limestone — as long as no quartz lies in between.

Flat bit, spherical buttons. In jointed rock concave (drop centre) instead, so the hole stays straight.

Spherical buttons, more flushing holes. At Mohs 7 it is carbide wear that sets the bit life.

Spherical for now. That layout forgives the most. Once we have the photo of the cuttings we fix it for good.

From question 02

Method and energy

DTH 3″–6″. We check the air volume and working pressure of your compressor against the hammer size before anything goes out.

Rotary tools with feed pressure. No percussion — instead attention on feed, torque and flushing.

Overburden string. Casing that advances with the bit, ring bit and casing shoe as one matched set.

Two routes compared. You get both methods side by side, with penetration rate and cost.

From question 03

Connection and articles

Top hammer drill steel R32 with matching bits and extension rods — anchor drilling and slope drilling rigs.

Top hammer drill steel T38 with bits and extension rods. The standard with the best availability.

Top hammer drill steel T45 for greater depths. Grease and check the joints regularly.

Top hammer drill steel T51 for bench drilling rigs. Partly made to order — tell us early what you need.

Hammer and bit as a pair, plus the matching drill string. The size in inches is enough for our proposal.

Connection still open. A photo of the threaded end or an article number — we identify it and tell you the stock.
