Drilling Equipment

Robit Sense Systems

Robit Sense measures hole deviation while drilling: S Sense for top-hammer surface rigs, M Sense as the manual variant.

  • Robit

Dimensions, threads and shanks are tabulated in full below. The data sheets download without registration.

Safety and blast result

Measuring hole deviation during drilling reduces the risk of flying rock in the blast.

Optimising the blast up front costs less than fixing deviation further down the chain in secondary breaking and crushing. Drilling accuracy determines the quality of the blast and with it the fragment size of the muckpile that goes into processing.

In Sweden, hole deviation measurement is compulsory for at least the first row of a blast pattern.

S Sense and M Sense

The Sense product family comes out of the research and development our Finnish partner Robit has done on rock and ground drilling. We distribute Robit drilling tools.

Robit developed a patented system that measures hole deviation during the percussive drilling process.

After an extensive test phase, S Sense for top-hammer surface drilling came to market at the beginning of 2017. Alongside it, M Sense is available as a manual variant, using the same know-how and technology developed for the S.

How the system works

  • The Sense probe sits inside the drill tool and travels the whole drill path with it.
  • Once final depth is reached, it determines hole path and hole depth while the drill string is pulled, and transmits the data.
  • On M Sense and on S Sense alike, the data is received and evaluated on a rugged tablet running a graphical interface.
  • Every deviation appears directly on the tablet. Feed pressure, rotation and other parameters can then be corrected for the rest of the drilling work.

Cloud service

With a SIM card inserted or over Wi-Fi, the data is also held in a cloud service.

For S Sense this means a central office can follow the drilling progress and plan the subsequent blast accordingly.

The cloud is also the central store for drilling data, patterns and plans. Authorised users can access it from anywhere.

Good preparation

Before a Sense system goes into service, the system and process linkages are planned. The Minroc Sense team works with the operators so that the data is genuinely used in day-to-day work.

An adjusted drilling process usually saves on fuel consumption, explosives and transport. With S Sense the measurement is integrated into the drilling process and does not have to be carried out afterwards.
