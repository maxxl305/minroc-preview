Drilling Equipment

Top Hammer Drill Steel

Shanks, drill rods and extension drill steel from Padley & Venables, plus tapered and threaded button bits from Robit in diameters from 33 to 152 mm.

  • Padley & Venables
  • Robit

Dimensions, threads and shanks are tabulated in full below. The data sheets download without registration.

Padley & Venables, Sheffield

PADLEY & VENABLES Ltd. in England has been making drill steel for the mineral extraction, tunnelling and mining industries for more than 100 years, certified to BS EN ISO 9001.

The P&V plant in Sheffield runs a pin furnace 7.5 m deep, one of the largest in Europe.

The supply range covers shanks for the common hand-held rock drills and for all hydraulic rock drills. Plus the matching drill rods in round or hex material, in various lengths, for anchor, exploration and face drilling.

Robit Rocktools, Finland

ROBIT Rocktools Ltd. in Finland has been making drill bits and drilling systems for more than 30 years, to the ISO 9001:2008 quality standard.

The Finnish range includes tapered and threaded button bits, with or without retrac, in diameters from 33 to 152 mm for anchor, exploration and face drilling.

Integral drill steels

Shank sizes 82.5 × 22 mm, 108 × 22 mm and 108 × 25 mm. Head diameters 24 to 45 mm, lengths 400 to 6,400 mm.

Taper drill steels

Taper angles 4°, 7°, 8°, 11° and 12°. With the matching bits in button or cross tungsten carbide tipped style.

Tunnelling equipment

Shanked rods, couplings and tunnelling rods in hex or round profile, male/female or male/male.

With the matching button bits and cross bits in all popular international thread forms.

Reaming equipment

Pilot adaptors with tungsten carbide tipped bits, cross or button style, tapered to take a reamer ring bit.

Plus domed reamer bits and combination pilot reaming bits.

Extension drill rods

Hexagon body and round body sections with all popular international thread forms.

Couplings as sleeve, semi-bridge, full-bridge or conversion type.

Male/female speedrods

Developed for use with high-powered air and hydraulic deep hole drilling machines, with most popular international thread forms.

Tubular drilling systems

Guide and extension drill tubes with most popular international thread forms, up to 6,400 mm long.
