Drill bit face designs

Which face design for what

  • Convex — suitable for most drilling applications, particularly hard, abrasive rock. Combines penetration rate and service life.
  • Flat — for general purpose drilling. The flat face design is considered effective in badly broken and hard ground as well as in softer formations that tend to over-drill.
  • Concave — an all-round face for soft as well as hard formations. The inward curve stabilises the bit in the hole and allows straighter holes.

Drill bit carbide designs

Which button shape for what

  • Dome (spherical) — for hard, abrasive rock and deep-hole drilling. Can be used in all applications.
  • Ballistic — a big chip producer. For less abrasive sedimentary deposits where speed decides. Less suited to badly broken ground and hard rock.
  • Parabolic (semi-ballistic) — higher penetration rate than the dome design, greater wear resistance than the ballistic. Less suited to broken ground.
