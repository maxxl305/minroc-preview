Drilling Equipment

Ventilation Systems

Spiral ducts from 150 to 2,400 mm diameter, flat ducts for forced ventilation, and the matching fittings and reducers.

  • GG

Dimensions, threads and shanks are tabulated in full below. The data sheets download without registration.

Spiral ducts

We supply spiral ducts from 150 to 2,400 mm diameter with all intermediate sizes, with 30, 50 or 75 mm spiral pitch or our 150 mm standard.

The fabric and the construction allow large diameters; the ducts run in extraction as well as in forced ventilation.

Fittings

Special pieces from “Y” to “T” are made with the radius of curvature you need, plus reducers to connect ducts of different diameters.

Flat ducts

For forced ventilation: flexible, PVC-coated carrier fabric for high-pressure ventilation in mining and tunnelling.

Connection types

  • Patented Easy-Joint system
  • Velcro fastening
  • Zip fastening
