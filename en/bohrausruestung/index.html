Drilling Equipment

The range

Ten product areas, from DTH equipment to ventilation, each with the manufacturer's dimension tables.

  • DTH Hammers

    DTH hammers from 3" to 24" with the matching drill bits and shanks, supplied as the official distributor for MINCON International Ltd. in Germany since 1987.

    Open group →
  • Top Hammer Drill Steel

    Shanks, drill rods and extension drill steel from Padley & Venables, plus tapered and threaded button bits from Robit in diameters from 33 to 152 mm.

    Open group →
  • RC — Reverse Circulation

    Mincon reverse circulation hammers: the MR range from 114 to 146 mm drilling diameter, plus its predecessor, the MX range, from 102 mm.

    Open group →
  • HDD Tools

    Mincon HDD pilot hammers from 77 to 194 mm outside diameter, and pneumatic pull reamers that widen a pilot hole to up to 356 mm.

    Open group →
  • Rotary Products

    Roller cone bits from 85 to 444.5 mm, drill tubes from 1,000 to 10,000 mm in length, drag bits, PCD bits and the Mincon drilling fluids and oils.

    Open group →
  • Pipe Umbrella Systems

    RoX+ pipe umbrella system from Robit: casing from 76.1 to 168.3 mm outside diameter, R32 to T45 threads, for top hammer and DTH drilling.

    Open group →
  • Casing Systems

    DTH RoX+ Single-Use and Multi-Use from Robit plus overburden drilling systems — casings from 114.3 to 1,220 mm outside diameter.

    Open group →
  • Rock Bolts

    Self-drilling anchors in R32, R38 and R51, SN and grouted rebar bolts from 20 to 50 mm and Tenso.Rig full-threaded bars in B500B and S670, plus bits, couplings, nuts and anchor plates.

    Open group →
  • Ventilation Systems

    Spiral ducts from 150 to 2,400 mm diameter, flat ducts for forced ventilation, and the matching fittings and reducers.

    Open group →
  • Robit Sense Systems

    Robit Sense measures hole deviation while drilling: S Sense for top-hammer surface rigs, M Sense as the manual variant.

    Open group →
