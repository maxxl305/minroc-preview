Drilling Equipment

Rock Bolts

Self-drilling anchors in R32, R38 and R51, SN and grouted rebar bolts from 20 to 50 mm and Tenso.Rig full-threaded bars in B500B and S670, plus bits, couplings, nuts and anchor plates.

Dimensions, threads and shanks are tabulated in full below. The data sheets download without registration.

Self-drilling anchors

Self-drilling anchors are injection piles with a continuous external thread. The system is based on the R32, R38 and R51 drill rod threads.

The threads are cold rolled over the entire rod length. Cold forming hardens and tempers the steel and raises the yield point. Every single rod therefore also passes a mechanical material test.

The continuous external thread carries the cuttings out of the hole. The large internal diameter of the anchor rod allows injection even in very long holes.

Minroc full-threaded bars

The Tenso.Rig anchor rods are solid bars in B500B or S670 steel with a left-hand thread profile over the complete length.

The rods can be cut to the length needed by hand on site. Extension to the system length is made with coupling sleeves.
