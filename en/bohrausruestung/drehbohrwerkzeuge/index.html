Mincon Rotacan

Mincon Rotacan designs and manufactures rotary drill bits, drill pipe and other drill string components in Canada.

Roller cone bits

We supply roller cone bits from 85 mm / 3 3/8“ to 444.5 mm / 17 1/2“.

Steel tooth bits (MT) work in soft to medium-hard formations, tungsten carbide insert bits (TCI) in medium-hard to very hard ones.

Bearing type, flushing and gauge protection can be specified; the bearing type determines sealing and wear protection.

Envirosafe Rock Drill Oil (RDO)

Mincon Envirosafe RDO is a rock drill oil for DTH hammers of all sizes. It is used in mining production, mineral exploration, horizontal directional drilling, quarrying and water well drilling.

RDO runs in all in-line lubricators. Its lubricating compounds resist being washed off in acidic water, salt water and foam, which reduces rust on the internal and external parts of the hammer even while the equipment stands idle.

A lubricant film on the internal parts prevents piston scuffing and heat build-up.

The viscosity lets the oil flow freely to the areas that need lubrication; switching between heavy and light oil as the seasons change is no longer necessary.

RDO is biodegradable. That makes it suited to water wells and monitor wells in sensitive areas, for instance next to streams and lakes.

Envirosafe Tri-Lube

Mincon Envirosafe Tri-Lube is formulated for blasthole drilling with rotary drills. The water-soluble lubricant extends the life of air bearing roller stabilisers, drill steel and rotary three-cone drill bits.

It is injected into the air or water line at 1.5 litres (3 US pints) per hour.

Tri-Lube reduces downhole torque, so joints break out more easily. It carries heat away from the cones and lubricates the cutting edge, which reduces bit retipping.

In the tank Tri-Lube prevents corrosion; injection pumps, seals and hoses are lubricated along the way.

Hole Control

Mincon Hole Control is a fluid for collaring and stabilising the bore hole. It can be used with air-rotary tri-cone bits, down-the-hole hammers and all types of top hammer drills, on surface and underground.

Hole Control reduces re-drilling caused by hole collapse, by transference between adjacent bores, or by cracks and voids in the rock. The collar sloughs less.

It stops cuttings from being reground, so bigger chips remain for sampling and penetration rates rise.

The cuttings pile stays sticky and neatly stacked and does not slough before the hole is loaded. Hole Control also suppresses dust, reduces wind-blown fines and lets blast holes be fully loaded.

Envirosafe Foamer ES

Mincon Envirosafe Foamer ES is a foaming agent for mineral exploration, oil and gas exploration and water well and geothermal drilling. It is also used as a drilling aid for monitoring wells.

Foamer ES develops tight foam immediately, compact bubbles with good lifting characteristics. Adding Mincon Hole Control makes the foam significantly stiffer where that is needed.

The suggested dosage under normal drilling conditions is 0.5 – 1.0 % by volume, and 2.0 – 3.0 % by volume where there is excessive water influx. It depends on the chemical make-up of the drilling water, the volume of water used and the depth of the hole.

PCD bits

PCD bits originate from the oil and gas industry. They are also used in geothermal, water well, civil engineering, geotechnical and quarry drilling.

The main body is made of tungsten carbide or steel. The buttons in the blades have a tungsten carbide base that absorbs the energy and a polycrystalline diamond cutting surface that stands up to hardness and abrasion.
