Machines

Micropile Rigs

Six crawler rigs for micropiles, anchors and tie rods, from the BIRDIE 200 for work on scaffolding to the EAGLE 1000 with 80 to 220 mm drilling diameter.

  • Ripamonti

BIRDIE 200

The BIRDIE 200 is a small drilling machine based on Ripamonti's SPEEDYDRILL. It produces micropiles, anchors, ties, small water wells and restoration holes, in foundation and civil engineering work as well as in the quarry.

It takes both the DTH hammer and the light hydraulic drifter. Dimensions and weights are laid out so the machine can be dismantled and used on scaffolding.

BIRDIE 500

The BIRDIE 500 is a small drilling machine based on Ripamonti's SPEEDYDRILL. It produces micropiles, anchors, ties, small water wells and restoration holes, in foundation and civil engineering work as well as in the quarry.

It takes both the DTH hammer and the light hydraulic drifter. Dimensions and weights are laid out so the machine can be dismantled and used on scaffolding.

BIRDIE 517

The BIRDIE 517 is a small drilling machine based on Ripamonti's SPEEDYDRILL. It produces micropiles, anchors, ties, small water wells and restoration holes, in foundation and civil engineering work as well as in the quarry.

It takes both the DTH hammer and the light hydraulic drifter. Dimensions and weights are laid out so the machine can be dismantled and used on scaffolding.

EAGLE 800

The EAGLE 800 is a compact crawler rig for holes from 80 to 220 mm in diameter. It drills micropiles, tie rods and holes for consolidation work.

The hydraulic system runs on proportional valves. They can be operated electrically by remote control or by hand, so the machine keeps working even if the radio system fails.

On the tool side it runs pneumatic hammers in rotary-percussive drilling, water-powered DTH hammers (Wassara), cased drilling systems (Supermaxbit, Ultramaxbit, Superjaws) and rotary drilling tools (tricone bits, trilama). Hydraulic hammers in rotary percussion set self-drilling anchor bars at drilling diameters up to 185 mm; in gallery work radial anchors and rivets are added.

The tilting undercarriage keeps the machine balanced while travelling and while loading and unloading. Radio control operates the rig from a distance, during drilling as well as during loading, unloading and positioning on site.

EAGLE 1000

The EAGLE 1000 is a compact crawler rig for holes from 80 to 220 mm in diameter, for micropiles, tie rods and consolidation drilling.

The hydraulic system runs on proportional valves. They can be operated electrically by remote control or by hand, so the machine keeps working even if the radio system fails.

On the tool side it runs pneumatic hammers in rotary-percussive drilling, water-powered DTH hammers (Wassara), cased drilling systems (Supermaxbit, Ultramaxbit, Superjaws) and rotary drilling tools (tricone bits, trilama). Hydraulic hammers in rotary percussion set self-drilling anchor bars at drilling diameters up to 185 mm; in gallery work radial anchors and rivets are added.

The remote control operates the rig from a distance, during drilling as well as during loading, unloading and positioning on site.

The drill mast is laid out for low component wear. The side guides can be replaced once they are worn.

The loader arm sits on a turntable base, is joined to the mast by a fifth wheel and telescopes out. That widens the area which can be drilled from one set-up.

MINIMEK 500

The MINIMEK 500 is a hydraulic crawler rig made up of a drilling unit and a separate control unit.

It is built for places that are hard to reach, in particular for work inside buildings. Typical jobs are underpinning, micropiles, tie rods and anchors.
