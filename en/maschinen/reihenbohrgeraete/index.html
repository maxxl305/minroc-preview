Row Drilling Units

Two row drilling units for the ornamental stone quarry, EX CAVA 1 and EX CAVA 2 by Ripamonti, mounted on the excavator.

  • Ripamonti

Excava are drilling kits designed to be mounted on an excavator. The reach of the boom makes dressing the stone block easier.

The kits can be built with one, two or four hydraulic drifters, depending on the excavator and the oil flow available. The rail for the line drill comes in lengths from 3 to 5 m.

EX CAVA 1

EX CAVA 2

EX CAVA 2 mounts on the excavator and squares blocks in the ornamental stone quarry. Its kinematics carry two drill rigs at once. The holes are drilled in line.

The machine is supported first and then finds the end line on the cut line. It requires an excavator over 20 tons.

All functions run on radio remote control. The operator does not stand at the machine and keeps the drill line in view.
