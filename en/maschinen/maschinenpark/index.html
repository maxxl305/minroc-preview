Lifting machines

MinLifter 795 T Evolution

The MinLifter 795 T Evolution is an articulated, four-wheel-drive tunnel work platform with two arms. Each arm lifts 750 kg. Basket floor height above ground is 9.5 m.

The two-arm lift-kit module 795 T is designed for installing light arches, charging holes at the face, mounting various anchors and placing wire mesh mats. Each arm can be controlled independently of the other.

Carrier units with different applications

  • As batch mixer – 5 m³
  • As dumper – 6/7 m³
  • As lifter – 500 kg
